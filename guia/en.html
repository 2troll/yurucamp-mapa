The real campsites of Yuru Camp△ (Laid-Back Camp)

Every campsite where Rin, Nadeshiko and the Outdoor Activities Club camp is a real place, mostly around Mt. Fuji in Yamanashi, Shizuoka and Nagano. Here are all 21, in anime order, with what happens there and what the real site is like.

🗺️ Open the interactive map

The free app also has 451 anime locations (houses, school, benches, onsen…), a GPS pilgrim mode that alerts you when you step into a scene, a camera that overlays the anime frame so you can recreate it, Japanese camping vocabulary and quizzes.

1. Kōan 浩庵キャンプ場

Episode: T1 · ep. 1 · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: Rin camps alone in November; Nadeshiko, newly moved, falls asleep on a bench looking for Mt. Fuji on the 1,000-yen bill.

What they learn: A small gesture (sharing some hot noodles) can spark a friendship.

What they eat: カレーめん · instant curry noodles

The real place: Campsites on the shore of Lake Motosu, with the same view of Mt. Fuji as the 1,000-yen bill. In winter, it drops below 0 °C at night.

Directions (35.4732, 138.5748)

2. Fumotoppara ふもとっぱら

Episode: T1 · ep. 3 & 11-12 · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: Nadeshiko shows up by surprise to return the Lake Motosu noodles to Rin with a nabe. Then, everyone's Christmas camping trip.

What they learn: Returning a favor with food you made yourself is very Japanese.

What they eat: 鍋 · nabe (hot pot) and すき焼き for Christmas

The real place: A huge meadow with Mt. Fuji right in front. It gets very windy: stake down your tent well.

Directions (35.3994, 138.5643)

3. Eastwood イーストウッドキャンプ場

Episode: T1 · ep. 4-5 · Who goes: Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: The club's (野クル) first winter camp without having all the gear.

What they learn: You can camp on a budget using things from home and a bit of ingenuity.

What they eat: 煮込みカレー · stewed curry

The real place: Forest campground in Yamanashi. Firewood is sold at reception.

Directions (35.7112, 138.6595)

4. Meseta de Takabotchi 高ボッチ高原

Episode: T1 · ep. 5 · Who goes: Rin Shima · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: Rin sets off alone by motorcycle to Nagano on the very day the club camps.

What they learn: The food you cook yourself tastes different from instant noodles.

What they eat: スープパスタ · soup pasta

The real place: You can see Lake Suwa and Mt. Fuji; at dawn there is often a sea of clouds. The road is usually closed in winter: check beforehand.

Directions (36.1352, 138.0370)

5. Lago Shibire 四尾連湖 水明荘

Episode: T1 · ep. 6-7 · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Sakura Kagamihara

What happens: Rin tries out a compact grill and Nadeshiko wants to make grilled meat. Sakura, her sister, drives them there.

What they learn: The grill won't light and a veteran camper helps them out: asking for help is totally fine.

What they eat: 焼肉 and 鱈鍋 · grilled meat and cod nabe

The real place: Small lake hidden between mountains, with the legend of a ghost. The car stays in the parking lot.

Directions (35.5306, 138.5187)

6. Monte Jinbagata 陣馬形山キャンプ場

Episode: T1 · ep. 9-10 · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki

What happens: Nadeshiko catches a cold and Rin goes to Kamiina alone; Nadeshiko and Chiaki act as her mobile "navigators".

What they learn: Plans go awry (closed roads), but friends guide you even when they're far away.

The real place: Free mountaintop campsite overlooking the Ina Valley. Reached via a narrow mountain road.

Directions (35.6730, 137.9832)

7. Ryūyō Kaiyō Kōen 竜洋海洋公園

Episode: T2 · ep. 2 · Who goes: Rin Shima

What happens: Rin rings in the new year alone, camping by the sea in Shizuoka.

What they learn: Chosen solitude is also a great New Year's Eve plan.

The real place: Auto campground next to the Pacific in Iwata.

Directions (34.6522, 137.8070)

8. Nagisa-en (lago Hamana) 渚園キャンプ場

Episode: T2 · ep. 3 · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara

What happens: Nadeshiko, visiting her grandmother's house, shows up by surprise where Rin is camping.

What they learn: Surprise camping and deep conversations by the fire.

What they eat: うなぎ · Lake Hamana eel

The real place: On the shore of Lake Hamana, near the large torii gate of Bentenjima.

Directions (34.6989, 137.6061)

9. Cabo Ōmama (lago Yamanaka) 大間々岬

Episode: T2 · ep. 5-6 · Who goes: Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: Chiaki, Aoi, and Ena plan their own camping trip with an onsen and ice cream.

What they learn: It's so cold their drinks freeze and firewood is no longer sold: winter prep is serious business.

The real place: Mt. Fuji on the other side of Lake Yamanaka, one of the coldest lakes in the area during winter.

Directions (35.4237, 138.8929)

10. Parque Nodayama 野田山健康緑地公園

Episode: T2 · ep. 7-8 · Who goes: Nadeshiko Kagamihara · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: Nadeshiko's first solo camping trip, after Rin teaches her everything.

What they learn: Being alone doesn't mean being isolated: she ends up sharing her dinner with two kids.

What they eat: ホイル焼き · vegetables roasted in aluminum foil

The real place: Fujikawa municipal park with a covered cooking area (炊事場).

Directions (35.1483, 138.5978)

11. Hosono Kōgen 細野高原ツリーハウス村

Episode: T2 · ep. 10 · Who goes: Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō, Minami Toba

What happens: The trip to Izu begins in Toba-sensei's van.

What they learn: The planned campsite cannot be used: they have to improvise a plan B.

The real place: Higashi-Izu Plateau, famous in autumn for its susuki (silver grass) fields.

Directions (34.7943, 139.0164)

12. Camp Koganezaki キャンプ黄金崎

Episode: T2 · ep. 11 · Who goes: Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō, Minami Toba, Rin Shima

What happens: The alternative campsite in Izu, after the onsen at sunset.

What they learn: A plan B can turn out better than plan A.

What they eat: Nadeshiko and Aoi's homemade dinner

The real place: In western Izu; the Koganezaki cliff turns golden at sunset (hence its name).

Directions (34.8427, 138.7656)

13. Darumayama Kōgen だるま山高原キャンプ場

Episode: T2 · ep. 12 · Who goes: Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō, Minami Toba, Rin Shima · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: Last night in Izu: surprise birthday for Nadeshiko and Aoi.

What they learn: Celebrating together in nature is the best gift.

What they eat: Surprise birthday cake

The real place: View of Mt. Fuji over Suruga Bay.

Directions (34.9744, 138.8544)

14. Shōji Lakeside 精進レークサイドキャンプ場

Episode: T3 · ep. 1 · Who goes: Rin Shima · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: Rin remembers how her grandfather got her into camping as a kid.

What they learn: Hobbies are inherited: grandfather gave her her first gear.

The real place: Lake Shōji is the smallest of the Fuji Five Lakes.

Directions (35.4934, 138.6073)

15. Apt Ichishiro アプトいちしろキャンプ場

Episode: T3 · ep. 3-5 · Who goes: Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Rin Shima, ayano

What happens: Nadeshiko waits and sets up camp while Rin and Ayano brave the Hatanagi road by motorcycle.

What they learn: Having everything ready for your tired friends is also a way of caring for them.

What they eat: 牛まつり · beef feast and bonfire

The real place: Next to the Ōigawa railway, where the train climbs using a rack system (Apt system, アプト式).

Directions (35.1668, 138.1496)

16. Mizugakiyama みずがき山自然公園

Episode: T3 · ep. 7-8 · Who goes: Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō

What happens: "Bus-camp": Chiaki, Aoi, and Ena go by bus while Nadeshiko is at the Ōigawa.

What they learn: Truth or exaggeration? Telling stories about camping is also part of camping.

What they eat: めしテロ · "food terrorism"

The real place: Beneath the granite rocks of Mt. Mizugaki, one of Japan's 100 famous mountains.

Directions (35.8957, 138.5730)

17. Garganta Ōyanagawa 大柳川渓流キャンプ場

Episode: T3 · ep. 9 · Who goes: Rin Shima

What happens: Rin goes camping alone again after a long time, right in cherry blossom season.

What they learn: Returning to what you love after a break from it.

The real place: Fujikawa Gorge with waterfalls and suspension bridges.

Directions (35.5045, 138.3990)

18. Río Sanogawa 佐野川河川公園

Episode: T3 · ep. 10 · Who goes: Chiaki Ōgaki

What happens: Chiaki finally tries solo camping.

What they learn: Even the club leader needs to try being on her own.

The real place: Riverside in Nanbu, Nadeshiko's hometown.

Directions (35.2516, 138.5037)

19. Kawaguchiko Tozawa 夢見る河口湖 コテージ戸沢センター

Episode: Movie (2022) · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō · 🗻 Fuji

What happens: As adults, the five of them reunite to build a campsite.

What they learn: High school dreams can come true when you grow up.

The real place: Cabins and campsite by Lake Kawaguchi.

Directions (35.5227, 138.7539)

20. Minobu Shizen no Sato みのぶ自然の里

Episode: Movie (2022) · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō

What happens: Appears in the movie featuring the girls as adults.

What they learn: Returning to the town where it all began.

The real place: Mountain campground in Minobu.

Directions (35.4719, 138.4033)

21. Ashiyasu 芦安キャンプサイト

Episode: Movie (2022) · Who goes: Rin Shima, Nadeshiko Kagamihara, Chiaki Ōgaki, Aoi Inuyama, Ena Saitō

What happens: Appears in the movie featuring the girls as adults.

What they learn: The usual friends are still there.

The real place: Mountain campsite in Minami-Alps, gateway to the Southern Alps.

Directions (35.6316, 138.3616)
